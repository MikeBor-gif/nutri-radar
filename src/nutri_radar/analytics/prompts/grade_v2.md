You are given the ingredient list of a food product.

Predict its Nutri-Score grade: a, b, c, d, or e.

What the grades typically look like in terms of composition:

a — a single whole food or a short list: vegetables, fruit, plain water,
    legumes, plain grains. No added sugar, no added fat, no additives.
b — a short list, lightly processed: yoghurt, plain bread, canned
    vegetables. Little added sugar or fat.
c — a moderate list with some added sugar, oil, or salt: cheeses,
    breakfast cereals, sauces.
d — a long list with clear added sugar or fat and several additives:
    biscuits, sweetened drinks, processed snacks.
e — sugar or fat is among the first ingredients, or the list is dominated
    by confectionery components: chocolate, sweets, chips, energy drinks.

Judge only from the ingredients: their order matters, the first ingredients
dominate the product by weight. You are NOT given nutrient values.

Answer with the grade only.
