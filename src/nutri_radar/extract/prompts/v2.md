Parse the food ingredient list into structured data.
Canonical names in ENGLISH, lowercase.

kind rules:
- "sugar": ANY form of sugar or syrup. Includes: sugar, glucose syrup,
  glucose-fructose syrup, corn syrup, high fructose corn syrup, molasses,
  dextrose, fructose, maltodextrin, invert sugar syrup, honey, treacle,
  caramel, malt extract, fruit juice concentrate.
- "sweetener": non-sugar sweeteners such as aspartame, sucralose, stevia,
  acesulfame k, sorbitol, xylitol.
- "additive": has an E-number, or is a colour / raising agent / stabiliser
  identified by a number (for example "red 40", "blue 1", "e330").
  Put the number in e_number.
- "preservative": preserving agents.
- "flavouring": flavourings and aromas only.
- "fat": oils and fats.
- "base": everything else, including flours, milk, water, salt, spices.

Allergens: plain names, no markup, no percentages.
If the text is unreadable or truncated, set unreadable to true.
